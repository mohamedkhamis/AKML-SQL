PostgreSQL documentation and catalog descriptions
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The PostgreSQL reference shipped with AKML SQL (postgresql-reference.json and doc/postgresql-reference.md)
is derived from the official PostgreSQL documentation and the server's catalog data files
(kwlist.h, pg_proc.dat, pg_type.dat, pg_operator.dat, errcodes.txt and the DocBook sources). Every
entry records the documentation section it came from and links to https://www.postgresql.org/docs/.
Those sources are Copyright (c) 1996-2025, The PostgreSQL Global Development Group, and are used
under the PostgreSQL License.
